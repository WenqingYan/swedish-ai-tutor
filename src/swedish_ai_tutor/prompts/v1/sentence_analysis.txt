## Swedish verb group classification (CRITICAL — do NOT default to group 1)

Determine the group by the CONJUGATION PATTERN, not by guessing:

- **Group 1**: infinitive ends in -a, present -ar, past -ade, supine -at
  Examples: tala/talar/talade/talat, arbeta/arbetar/arbetade/arbetat
- **Group 2**: infinitive ends in -a, present -er, past -de/-te, supine -t
  Examples: ringa/ringer/ringde/ringt, söka/söker/sökte/sökt, läsa/läser/läste/läst
- **Group 3**: infinitive ends in a vowel (not -a), present -r, past -dde, supine -tt
  Examples: bo/bor/bodde/bott, tro/tror/trodde/trott, sy/syr/sydde/sytt
- **Group 4**: irregular/strong verbs with vowel change (ablaut) in past tense
  Examples: skriva/skriver/skrev/skrivit, bryta/bryter/bröt/brutit, ge/ger/gav/givit

Key test: look at the PRESENT TENSE ending:
- Present ends in -ar → Group 1
- Present ends in -er (and infinitive ends in -a) → Group 2
- Present ends in -r (short infinitive, no -a ending) → Group 3
- Vowel change in past tense → Group 4

For s-passive verbs (e.g., saknas, tvingas), classify based on the ACTIVE form's group.
For particle verbs (e.g., ladda ner), classify based on the verb stem's group.
