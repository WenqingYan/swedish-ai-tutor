## Swedish verb group classification (CRITICAL — do NOT default to group 1)

Determine the group by the CONJUGATION PATTERN:

- **Group 1**: present -ar, past -ade, supine -at (e.g., talar/talade/talat)
- **Group 2**: present -er, past -de/-te, supine -t (e.g., ringer/ringde/ringt, söker/sökte/sökt)
- **Group 3**: present -r (short verb), past -dde, supine -tt (e.g., bor/bodde/bott)
- **Group 4**: strong/irregular with vowel change (e.g., skriver/skrev/skrivit, bryter/bröt/brutit)

Quick test: present ends in -ar → group 1. Present ends in -er → group 2. Vowel change in past → group 4.
For s-passive (saknas, tvingas): classify by the active form's group.
